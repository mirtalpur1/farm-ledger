Assumptions & Notes
  • Shared Inputs: Fertilizer, Seed, and Pesticide costs are split 50/50.
  • Combined Misc Expenses: Any additional miscellaneous costs (canal cleaning, guard salary, etc.) are also split 50/50 — half by the owner, half by the sharecropper.
  • Sharecropper Costs: Ploughing & Labour are 100% the sharecropper's responsibility, covered by their profit share.
  • Data Entry: Enter total costs first. If the sharecropper paid anything, check the box and enter those specific amounts.
